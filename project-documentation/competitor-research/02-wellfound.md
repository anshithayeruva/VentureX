# Wellfound — research notes (researched 2026-11-23)

Positioning (directly observed): **startup hiring / AI recruiting platform**, not a fundraising platform. Formerly AngelList Talent, spun out and rebranded Nov 2022 (Wikipedia). Users: job seekers ("candidates") and companies ("recruiters"). 27,000+ startups hiring, "10M candidates", "500M+ profiles" in Reach. There is **no investor portal and no fundraising workflow**; fundraising/investing moved to AngelList (see 07-angellist.md).

## Actual user model
- **Company side (founders acting as employers)**: create/claim a company profile → post jobs (free, unlimited, built-in ATS or sync Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/Workable) → manage applicants & conversations → optional **Reach** (AI sourcing agents: evaluate candidates, write personalised outreach, send from your Gmail/Outlook; Free 20 reveals/3 agents, Starter $135/seat/mo, Growth $199/seat/mo, Scale custom+SSO) → optional **Autopilot** ($500/mo/role + 10% placement fee, dedicated recruiter delivers 5–10 candidates/week). Job Ads from $200.
- **Candidate side**: profile beyond résumé (projects), AI matching ("Matched to you · today"), apply directly to founders, recruiters find you ("Get discovered").
- **Company profile fields** (help article 711, directly listed): Company name, High concept (one-liner), Logo, Company size, Markets, Slug, Locations, Company type, Website, Blog URL, Social links; plus people/team page, culture & benefits, photos, video, testimonials, exits, "Closed/Operating" status. **Community-generated profiles**: a company profile is auto-created when employees/advisors/investors add it to their own profile; can be claimed.
- **Public company page sections** (directly observed on /company/wefunder): Overview · People · Culture and benefits · Funding; badges "Top Investors", "YC Funded", "Growth Stage", B2B/B2C; Founders block; **Funding block: Valuation, number of rounds, latest round**; "Total raised"; Markets; "People also viewed"; responsiveness badge ("Top 10% of responders" — seen in search snippet for this page).

## Relevance to VentureX
Low as a direct competitor. Useful patterns: (1) startup profile doubles as an employer/brand page (team, culture, funding summary); (2) **responsiveness badges** (response-time ranking) to reward fast replies — applicable to investors/founders on VentureX; (3) community-generated, claimable profiles; (4) agentic AI outreach that runs from the user's own inbox; (5) trust/AI audit centre (published AI-bias audit of résumé screening — relevant if VentureX uses AI to rank startups).
