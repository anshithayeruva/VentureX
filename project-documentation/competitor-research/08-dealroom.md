# Dealroom — research notes (researched 2026-11-23)

Positioning (directly observed): "Intelligence for tech ecosystems — the source of record on startups, founders and funding, plus the signals on who is rising next. For your team and your AI agents." Data platform (3M+ companies, 100K+ investors) used by VCs, corporates, governments, universities; plus an **Ecosystem Platform** for governments/accelerators (100+ partner ecosystems, 40+ countries). Strong in Europe; India appears in live data (Bangalore, Pixxel).

## Investor / analyst side
- Views (illustrative tour): **Search · Companies · Sector · Locations · Investors · Deals · Signals · Newsfeed · API**; "Top companies" table with **Signal score (0–100)**, HQ, team size + growth %, funding + growth %, key people; "Hot sectors" (momentum, startups count, $ raised, growth %), "Hot startups this week", "Hot locations" (VC growth by city — Bangalore +64%).
- **Investors**: ranked by **match score** (e.g. Sequoia 98), investor profile with AUM, top exits, top holdings, selected LPs; **side-by-side investor comparison** (exits, unicorns, early-stage, global, follow-on, AI focus).
- Sector deep-dives (amount raised, companies, unicorns, sub-segments); market maps & lists; rankings; dashboards (VC, exits, multiples); founder networks/"mafias"; **founder alerts**; news, Daily Deal email, events, startup jobs.
- Exports (credits per seat), business email credits (Premium Plus), CRM integration, **MCP** for Claude/Cursor/VS Code (Premium Plus), REST API add-on, SSO (Enterprise), analyst support.

## Founder side
- Founders can **claim their company profile free**: sign up with a company-domain email (domain must match company website to verify) → claim → edit with pencil icon (logo, name, address, financials, funding rounds, team, revenue, product). **Submissions are manually verified before publication.** "Add your entity" if no profile exists (name, website, LinkedIn). Founder free package / trial mentioned (inconsistent sources). (knowledge.dealroom.co/knowledge/creating-claiming-editing-company-profile; dealroom.co/faq)
- No founder outreach, CRM or deck-sharing workflow; value for founders = visibility to investors/corporates and investor research.

## Ecosystem Platform
- White-label ecosystem dashboards for cities/governments (e.g. pittsburgh.dealroom.app): map/table of enterprise value, VC invested, startups, unicorns by location; partner hub.

## Relevance to VentureX
- **Signal / momentum score** on startups (team growth, funding growth) and **investor match score** — VentureX already has fit scores; Dealroom shows the value of a visible, sortable momentum signal driven by *updates over time* (VentureX stores a single snapshot of traction).
- **Investor comparison** view and investor track-record fields (exits, holdings) — VentureX investor profiles have none.
- **Domain-verified claiming + manual verification of submitted funding data** — a trust model VentureX lacks.
- Ecosystem dashboards: possible B2B angle for Indian state startup missions / incubators (Startup India, state nodal agencies) — long-term.
