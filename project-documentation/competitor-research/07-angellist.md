# AngelList — research notes (researched 2026-11-23)

Positioning (directly observed): "The financial system for private markets — a portfolio of businesses that move capital through private markets." No longer a startup–investor discovery network (talent spun out as Wellfound: "WellFound is no longer a part of the AngelList portfolio"). Businesses: **Funds** (fund administration for venture funds, Rolling Funds, Scout Funds, SPVs/syndicates — "25,000 syndicates & funds", "$200B+ assets"), **USVC** (registered fund for retail investors), **Meridian** (wealth platform for accredited investors: discovery, diligence, portfolio), **Nova** (banking/payments network, coming soon), **Rollups.com** (Roll Up Vehicles for founders), **AngelList Link** (MCP server for GPs).

## Founder side
- **Roll Up Vehicles (RUVs)** — Rollups.com: founder creates a private SPV so up to **250 accredited angels** invest through **one invite link**; AngelList handles entity formation, capital collection, accreditation, KYC, e-signatures; shows as **one line on the cap table**; no lead/stand-in investor needed; only visible via the founder's link. Used to raise from customers/communities (case studies: Efani raised $1M from customers). India variant free for founders. (blog.angellist.com/introducing-roll-up-vehicles; angellist.com/startups/ruv)
- Founders otherwise interact as **portfolio companies** of GPs on AngelList (receiving SPV/fund investments) — no founder fundraising marketplace, profile, investor search or CRM.

## Investor side
- **GPs / syndicate leads**: launch SPVs (506(b) private or 506(c) public, can submit deal to AngelList network for exposure), back-office (formation, filings, K-1s), **branded data room + digital subscriptions** (KYC/AML, accreditation checks), dashboard to monitor portfolio company **valuations**, distributions handled on liquidity events. Rolling Funds (quarterly subscriptions), Scout Funds. Fund admin: daily reconciled financials, automated capital calls/distributions via built-in banking, LP onboarding (150k+ pre-verified LPs), tax and audit support, migrations, "agentic fund management".
- **Data Room** (free): branding, granular permissions, real-time notifications, document tracking, **page-by-page analytics**, centralised reporting, integrated with digital subscriptions.
- **AngelList Link** (new): MCP server — "ask questions about your fund from any AI tool and get a verified answer"; **request the source behind any number**; daily financials; works in Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf.
- **LPs / accredited investors — Meridian** (directly observed): apply + verify accreditation → profile with preferences → **"matched opportunities"** (startup SPVs and funds aligned with background and interests), "premier access" with accessible minimums, closing in minutes; manage all private-market holdings in one place. No platform fee for browsing; existing AngelList investors log in with same credentials.

## Relevance to VentureX
- VentureX does not and should not do regulated fund admin/SPVs. But two patterns matter:
  1. **Closing the loop**: AngelList turns "interest" into money movement (subscriptions, KYC, cap table line). VentureX stops at a deal room; a light "commitment" step (soft-circle amount + status, documents signed elsewhere) would bridge to the portfolio module.
  2. **Matched opportunities from investor preferences** (Meridian) and **verifiable AI answers** (Link: every number traceable) — same principle as Visible verification; apply to VentureX AI outputs.
- Community round / RUV concept: Indian equivalents are regulated differently (no direct analogue; would need legal review) — not recommended for VentureX scope.
