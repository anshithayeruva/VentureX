# Gust — research notes (researched 2026-11-23)

Positioning (directly observed): "Startup smarter — the best place to start, grow, and fund your venture." Two businesses: (1) founder side — free Gust Company Profile + paid **Gust Launch** (Delaware incorporation, cap table, legal docs, SAFEs/notes, options/409A); (2) **Gust Group Investor Platform** — SaaS deal-flow management for angel groups, VCs and accelerators (350+ groups, 44k investors, 197 countries, 27k applications in 2022). Claims 850k+ startups, 85k investment professionals; 800+ investor orgs/programs in directory; 300 accelerators, 750 angel groups.

## Founder side
- **Gust Company Profile**: one place for "your pitch to your cap table, with sharing and access control". Public page (directly observed): tabs **Overview · Executive Summary · Financials**; fields Stage (e.g. "$50M+ in TTM Revenue"), Industry, Location, Currency, Founded, Employees, Incorporation type, Website, Company summary, Team (name, title, past company). Upsell banner: free **Corporate Diligence Report** ("don't lose investors over fixable corporate issues").
- **Publishing** (help 211): published profiles appear in a directory with a **limited view**; contact info, executive summary, documents, financials and pitch deck stay private. Only investors in groups licensing the Group Investor Platform can **request full access** — founder grants access.
- **Finding/applying to investors** (210/213/216): search icon → filters **Location, Industry, Keyword**; read the org's profile; "Apply" re-uses the Company Profile (no re-entry); track applications in one place; re-apply allowed (225). Advice: local investors fund most.
- **After applying** (223/222): weeks–months; groups work in cycles. Founder notified of status changes; "Inactive"/"Declined" = pass, founder can **withdraw profile** so group can't view it; founder notified when an investor sets an **interest level** (activity summary) but can't contact them directly.
- **Startup tools**: Benchmark your venture ("how much you can raise, which investors to target, how to improve"), **co-founder equity split tool** (cofounders.gust.com), incorporation, cap table modelling, SAFE/convertible note issuance with e-signatures + board/shareholder consents → reflected on cap table, option plan + 409A, legal agreements (IP assignment, NDAs, contractor/advisor), $100k+ partner perks qualified by profile data, accelerator applications.
- **Mission Control**: weekly office hours (David S. Rose), expert sessions (financial model teardowns, pitch reviews), vetted service network (fractional CFO, lawyers).
- Pricing (verified): Start $450/yr (incorporation, 83(b), digital cap table, registered agent); Accelerate $1,250/yr (+ unlimited SAFEs/notes/NDAs, cap table modelling, legal network); Raise $3,500/yr (+ option plan, 409A, option issuance/exercise). Company profile & investor applications: free.

## Investor side (Group Investor Platform)
- **Onboarding** (338): group approved by Gust → admin receives "Welcome to Gust!" email → sign up (name, city, password) → "Welcome to Your New Investor Workspace" → configure group, invite members (327), import member data.
- **Receiving applications** (228): admin toggles **Application Editor** on; founders find the group in the directory (keyword/industry/location) or via a private application link on the group's own site (can be unlisted); groups can invite founders from their network; filter applicants by investment preferences.
- **Deal pipeline** (367): admin-defined **stages** in three categories — Active (default "New"; typical New > Screened > Considering > Pitching > Due Diligence, drag to reorder), Completed (default "Invested"), Inactive (default "Declined"; custom e.g. junk, duplicate, on hold). Bulk-move with checkboxes.
- **Interest levels, ratings & reviews** (230): members set interest level — Observing / Considering / Funds Committed / Invested (+ amount) or Not Interested; admins/deal leads can set on behalf of members; levels and amounts can be private; setting interest **subscribes** member to the deal (Daily Activity Summary email). Separate ratings & reviews for screening.
- **Communication** (227): Deal Discussions (reply-all, targeted audiences), Group Discussions, Announcements; email integration; per-member thread settings.
- **Due diligence**: secure deal **data rooms** with access control; event scheduling with email/calendar integration.
- **Syndication** (232/349): **Co-Investment** — share a deal with another Gust group with a message; receiving group sees full profile and your group's aggregate interest; track financial commitments across syndicate.
- Integrations: Zapier; email/calendar.
- **Accelerators** (345): Gust Accelerator Platform — organisation profile → programs as cohorts → application editor → evaluators/judging (also used for business plan competitions).

## Relevance to VentureX
- Strong pattern: **group/team deal flow** (shared pipeline, member interest levels with committed amounts, ratings, discussions, syndication) — VentureX investor side is single-user today.
- **Limited public view + request-full-access** model is a clean privacy pattern (VentureX shares full published project with all investors).
- **Investor-defined application form** and private application links.
- **Corporate Diligence Report** / incorporation readiness — analogous to VentureX readiness but legal/corporate.
- Founder notified of investor interest level and status changes; can withdraw.
- Weakness: dated UX, slow group cycles, no AI.
