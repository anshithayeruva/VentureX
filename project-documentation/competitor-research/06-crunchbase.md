# Crunchbase — research notes (researched 2026-11-23)

Positioning (directly observed): "Private company data & predictive intelligence — before it's news, it's in Crunchbase." A data/intelligence product for investors, sales/GTM teams, analysts, wealth managers and entrepreneurs; not a two-sided fundraising marketplace. Now positioned as **"private market data, now headless"** — data + signals for users and their AI agents (MCP in ChatGPT/Claude). Claims 80M users, 39B live signals, 30M+ verified updates/yr, 15M+ predictions refreshed weekly, "84% of real-world funding rounds predicted".

## User model
- **Searchers (investors, sales, analysts)**: Advanced Search, **AI Search**, **Crunchbase Scout** (ask natural-language questions like "Which fintech companies may raise funding soon?"), saved lists & saved searches (Pro up to 100 each, Business unlimited), **market maps**, list workflow with **stages, notes and tags**, alerts on signals, Chrome extension, exports (Pro 2,000 rows/mo, Business 5,000), CRM/workflow integrations on Business (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, Outreach), SSO and team management (Business), MCP tool calls for agents.
- **Data in a profile**: firmographics, financial & funding data (rounds, investors, total funding), people & contacts, **growth & heat scores**, growth/news/product insights, market & micro-industry insights, competitors & similar companies, **investor insights and likely investors**, **current valuation estimates**, forecasts (funding, growth, acquisition, IPO, remain-private, closure, layoffs).
- **Live signals feed** (directly observed): funding round, growth insight (e.g. PhonePe revenue "₹3,918 crore, +22.2% YoY"), acquisition prediction ("very likely"), product launch, leadership hire.
- **Companies/founders**: no claim button; any registered user can edit any profile (community-edited); **verified employees** (logged in with company-domain email) get exclusive edit rights to the Overview section (lock icon fields); founders add closed funding rounds (feeds Total Funding), founders, people; Crunchbase also offers a form where its team creates/updates the profile; inaccuracies via support email. Profile type can be changed to "Investment firm" for investors.

## Founder vs investor relevance
- Founders use Crunchbase mainly to **research investors** ("investor insights and likely investors", investor portfolios) and keep their own profile accurate for visibility; no outreach, CRM pipeline or deck sharing for founders.
- Investors use it for **sourcing and screening** (search, AI predictions, heat scores, lists with stages/notes/tags, alerts, CRM sync) — deal-flow tracking is light (lists), not a full pipeline.

## Relevance to VentureX
- **Predictive signals** (likely to raise / growth / closure) and **"likely investors"** for a company = data network effects VentureX can't match, but VentureX can do a narrower version from its own data: "startups likely to fit you that just updated traction", "investors who invested in similar startups on VentureX".
- **Natural-language AI search** over startups (Scout) — VentureX Discover is filter-based only.
- **Saved searches + alerts** ("notify me when a new startup matches this search").
- **Verified-employee editing** (company-domain email verification) — VentureX has no verification of founders/companies.
- Lists with stages/notes/tags is the minimum investor deal-tracking bar.
