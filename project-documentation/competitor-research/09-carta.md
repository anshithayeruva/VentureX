# Carta — research notes (researched 2026-11-23)

Positioning (directly observed; carta.com redirected this session to the APAC site carta.com/sg/en/): "Private capital, intelligently connected." Three suites — **Equity Suite** (cap table, SAFEs, valuations, compensation, liquidity, compliance; free **Carta Launch** for early founders), **Fund ERP** (fund admin, fund tax, forecasting, portfolio valuations, SPVs, loan ops, **Deal CRM**, LP CRM, LP portfolio analytics), **Carta Law** (AI-native law firm). "Trusted by 50,000 companies", "$335B raised by companies on Carta", "85% of unicorns". AI: **Carta Plugins for Claude** (Cap Table, Investors, CRM plugins), agentic monitoring.

## Founder side
- **Carta Launch (free)**: cap table, stakeholder logins, electronic share issuance/transfers, **fundraising scenario modelling** + funding benchmarks, SAFE generation (Carta/YC/custom templates), on-platform exercises & wires, equity split tool, Carta Insights (salary/equity data), SAFE financing tool, burn-rate calculator, support.
- **SAFE financings**: generate SAFEs, e-signatures, multiple currencies, issuance only after funds arrive, real-time status, auto-update cap table; **SAFE insights tool** — market data on valuation caps, term distributions, number of investors to negotiate terms.
- Paid tiers (Core→Scale): investor management, equity reporting, HRIS/payroll integration, **data rooms**, board meetings, advanced scenario modelling, valuations (IFRS 13/409A), IFRS 2/ASC 718 reporting, SSO.
- No investor discovery/outreach for founders.

## Investor side
- **Deal CRM**: auto-sync email & calendar interactions; notes, stakeholders, history per deal; mobile app; **AI-powered research + context-aware assistant**; **relationship maps** (strongest path into a deal); customisable pipelines with stages across multiple deals in a spreadsheet-style workspace; real-time reporting; "who is doing what" updates; replaces "Monday deal meeting" prep.
- **Portfolio Valuations**: collect/standardise portfolio data, methodologies (public comps, DCF), IPEV/IFRS 13 templates, audit support, connected to cap tables + PortCo financials + fund admin.
- Fund admin, fund tax, forecasting, SPVs, LP CRM ("predictive engagement"), LP portfolio analytics.
- Plugins for Claude: query live books ("Show me Fund II's Schedule of Investments").

## Relevance to VentureX
- Carta owns the post-term-sheet stack (cap table, SAFEs, valuations) — VentureX shouldn't build this, but can **link out/integrate** and borrow two ideas: (1) **round modelling/dilution calculator** for founders (pre/post-money, SAFE conversion) — cheap, high-value founder tool; (2) **market term benchmarks** (valuation caps by stage) — VentureX can surface its own aggregate data once it has volume.
- Deal CRM patterns: email/calendar sync, relationship maps, AI assistant over the pipeline.
- India note: Carta's APAC presence; Indian startups commonly use local cap-table tools (e.g. Qapita, EquityList) and instruments like CCPS/CCDs — VentureX's instrument list already includes "Convertible note"; consider India-specific instruments (CCPS, iSAFE) in criteria/portfolio.
