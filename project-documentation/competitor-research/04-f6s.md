# F6S — research notes (researched 2026-11-23)

Positioning (directly observed): "F6S is where companies grow together" — a founder community (6.7M members) whose main jobs are **applying to accelerators, grants and funding opportunities** (16k open calls), **software deals** (AWS, Google, Microsoft, ChatGPT — "save up to 80%"), **startup jobs** (13k) and recruiting (530k job seekers). Organisations (accelerators, funds, corporates, governments) use **F6S Pipeline**, a deal-flow CRM with application forms and team evaluation.

## Founder side
- **Sign up**: email or other providers ("Continue with email", "Continue another way"), free.
- **Startup page**: founders section (co-founders), markets, location, team & product videos, recommendations from third parties (founder sends a request; recommender writes via emailed link — help 237).
- **Funding discovery** (directly observed, /investment-funds): directory of investment funds (193), accelerators, angel groups, grants, contests, corporate pilots, events. Filters: Type, Location, Markets, Investment, Invest in Country; sort Open now / Popular / Closest; card shows name, location, cheque per team (e.g. "$250k per team", "INR50m per team"), equity (e.g. "5-10% equity"), **"Pitch"** and "More info". "Featured" and "Close to you" carousels; "List Me Here" for organisations.
- **Applying** (help 62–73, 212, 169): any co-founder can apply to an open call; the program's **custom application form** (questions, markets, location, team/product video, team members who must confirm); edit after submission; post **"Updates"** to the program team after submitting (help 64); delete application; switch which startup you apply with; one profile reused across applications.
- Perks/discounts tied to membership; jobs board; recruiting.

## Investor / organisation side — F6S Pipeline (CRM)
- Used by "startup programs, investors, corporates and governments" (help 88) to: capture leads from **email, web, application forms, spreadsheets**; communicate inside the team and with prospects; analyse deal flow; find who-knows-whom (relationship intelligence); track deal flow together.
- **Pipelines** (89): named collections (e.g. "AI/ML Investment Leads", "2018 Startup Program Applications"); companies auto-added from application pages, from email, manually, or by **F6S scouting analysts** (Scouting Portal).
- **Table view** (93–102, 186–191): spreadsheet-like; add/hide/reorder/resize columns; column types Tags, Files, Notes, Incorporated, Founded, Investors & Affiliated (cap-table-like list of funds/accelerators/grants/mentors with investment details, confirmation email sent to the investor), Source; sort; filter by pipeline; **full-text search**; exclude markets/locations/statuses; saved & **shared custom table views**; pin pipelines.
- **Evaluation** (103, 193–194): detail page per startup — notes, **score**, print as brochure, arrow through the filtered list; find applications scored by an assigned evaluator / not yet scored.
- **Roles & permissions** (123–152): Owner, Evaluator (score, notes, see other judges' notes, add/copy/share startups; no export), Specific Evaluator, Viewer; per-pipeline and global permissions.
- **Gmail extension** (115–122): add incoming email to a pipeline, see who in your team is talking to a founder, change status, create statuses from Gmail.
- Program pages: accelerator/contest/event pages with application forms (copyable to a new cohort).

## Relevance to VentureX
- **Program/open-call model**: investors (or accelerators) publish an "open call" with their own application questions; founders apply with a reusable profile and post updates afterwards. VentureX only has free-form intro requests.
- **Team evaluation with scoring rubrics, assigned evaluators and roles** — collaborative screening, missing in VentureX.
- **Email-to-pipeline capture** (Gmail extension) and spreadsheet import — investors' deal flow doesn't start on the platform.
- **Third-party recommendations/references** on a startup profile — social proof.
- Perks marketplace as a founder acquisition lever (OpenVC, Gust and F6S all do this).
- Weakness: noisy, very broad marketplace; little AI; dated UX.
